Getting here

Where is "here"? Bumble Bee, Arizona: a dot on the map roughly an hour north of Phoenix off I-17, then down a dirt road until your phone gives up on you. Bumble Bee Ranch is at the end of that road. If you hit Cleator, you went too far. If you hit Crown King, you went way too far and should stay for a beer.

Accommodations

Camping. Acres of flat-ish desert for tents and RVs. No hookups: bring your own water, power, and dignity (optional). The road in is dirt, but it's fine for vans, smaller motorhomes, trailers, and, we have personally witnessed this, a Honda Civic. If a Civic can make it, so can your Sprinter.

Soft people. The nearest town, store, and actual bed is Black Canyon City, 11 miles from the venue. Gas, ice, beer, and a motel mattress. Black Canyon City ↗

Nearby sites (if you're visiting)

Cleator Yacht Club

A nautical-themed bar in the middle of the desert, in a near-ghost town with about eight permanent residents, and no water anywhere near it. There are no yachts. There is no club. There is a bar, and it has been serving dusty ATV riders and confused tourists for decades. Wear your captain's hat. Ask where the boats are docked. The whole town went up for sale in 2020, so if you've always wanted to be an admiral, now's your chance.

Crown King

An old gold-mining town high in the Bradshaw Mountains, 28 miles west of I-17 up a road that will shake your fillings loose. Tiny, weird, and cooler than the desert floor in every sense. Worth the drive if your vehicle and your kidneys can take it.
