How to register (read it twice)

$130 per rider · cash or check
  1. Pay $130 per rider. Cash or check. Not Venmo. Not "I'll get you at camp."
    • Check payable to: Roca Roja Bicycles
    • Memo line: SSAZ + the name(s) of everyone you're paying for
      e.g. "SSAZ + Jane Doe, John Doe"
  2. Include a note in the envelope with every registrant's name.
  3. Include your email address on the note, NEATLY WRITTEN. Block letters.
  4. +1s are fine. To add someone who isn't riding so they can take part in the food, send an additional $50 per non-rider and put their name on the note (and in the memo if paying by check).
  5. Put it all in an envelope, seal it, stamp it, and mail it to: Flat Tire Bike Shop, 6061 E Cave Creek Rd Ste 7, Cave Creek, AZ 85331.

Print it. Block letters. If we can't read your email we can't email you, and if we can't email you, you're uninvited. We will not guess whether that's a "u" or a "v." We will just drink your $130.

Example: two riders and one non-rider = $130 + $130 + $50 = $310, memo "SSAZ + Jane Doe, John Doe, Pat Doe".
